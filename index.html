WHAT A LESSON CAN BECOME

Learn → Watch → Explore → Play → Practise → Assess

1

📖 Learn

Clear concept explanation with alternative ways to understand it.

2

🎥 Watch

Short lesson videos or animations, followed by a thinking prompt.

3

🔬 Explore

Manipulate a simulation and see what changes and why.

4

🎮 Play

A game uses the same concept as its actual mechanic.

5

✍️ Practise

Guided questions with hints, steps and explanatory feedback.

6

🏆 Master

Assessment checks whether the learner can apply the idea independently.
